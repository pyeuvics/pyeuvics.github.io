# Design status

This page will distinguish publication lifecycle, claim type, evidence type,
and validation state. The labels below demonstrate the controlled vocabulary;
they do **not** assign a status to EUVICS, pyEUVICS, a document, or a result.

## Controlled vocabulary preview

<div class="status-list" aria-label="Controlled status vocabulary examples">
  <span class="status-badge">Draft</span>
  <span class="status-badge">Approval Pending</span>
  <span class="status-badge">Design Target</span>
  <span class="status-badge">Calculated</span>
  <span class="status-badge">Simulated</span>
  <span class="status-badge">Reference</span>
  <span class="status-badge">Validated</span>
  <span class="status-badge">Unvalidated</span>
  <span class="status-badge">Superseded</span>
  <span class="status-badge">Released</span>
</div>

`Released` does not mean `Validated`. A `Design Target` is not evidence of
achievement. Future badges require exact source evidence, visible scope, and
nearby limitations.
