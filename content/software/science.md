# pyEUVICS science and conventions

In an assembled build, approved source material defines electron energy as kinetic or total,
collision angle between propagation vectors, observation angle relative to the
electron direction, units and width conventions, and every applicable model
assumption and limitation.

This website does not implement pyEUVICS equations or calculations. The local
preview contains this boundary statement; production assembly adds links to the
approved source pages and their provenance.
